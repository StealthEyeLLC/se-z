## Interpretation

`PRESERVE` and `MECHANICAL_RENAME` entries can be measured directly in Phase 1. `CANONICAL_ADAPTATION_REQUIRED` preserves the proven core but needs a narrow target adapter. Deferred entries are assigned to 0.1A, 0.1B, or 0.1C and are not represented as completed behavior. `REMOVE_FROM_ACTIVE_RUNTIME` preserves historical tests and managed-credential hygiene while preventing source policy from silently becoming the final raw-output contract.
